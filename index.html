About Us

At Accelerated Information (or Accelmation), we understand that business data, especially big data, is only valuable if it can be transformed into meaningful information.

As a seasoned IT consulting firm, our primary objective is to empower your business to grow faster and operate more efficiently by leveraging our IT solutions to boost revenue and reduce operational costs.

Our Formula: Data + Automation + Analytics = Information

Together, we can make your business information flow faster—accelerating your decision-making process by ensuring you have the right information at the right time.
